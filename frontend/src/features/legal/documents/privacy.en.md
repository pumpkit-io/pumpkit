This Privacy Policy explains which personal data the Operator, INCEPTIONAL S.R.L., processes when you use Pumpkit at pumpkit.io, why, with whom, for how long, and how you can exercise your rights over it.

It covers only the hosted service at pumpkit.io. Pumpkit's code is open source, and anyone can run their own copy. The Operator does not run those self-hosted instances, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to them: whoever runs an instance is responsible for the data on it.

This Privacy Policy is available in English and Italian. Both versions are equally valid.

## What data the Operator processes, and why {#data}

Each category below says what the data is, what the Operator uses it for, and the legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) that allows it.

### Account and profile {#data-account}

- **What:** your email address, your name, and the profile picture you upload, if any.
- **Why:** to create your account, sign you in and show your profile.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Google identity {#data-google}

- **What:** if you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier, the profile Google shares with Pumpkit (name, email address and picture) and the sign-in token Google issues, stored encrypted.
- **Why:** to sign you in with your Google account.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Session and Magic link records {#data-sessions}

- **What:** for each Session, when it started and when it expires, with the IP address and browser user agent it was created from. For each Magic link, the email address it was sent to, when it was sent and used, and the IP address and user agent that requested it.
- **Why:** to keep you signed in, and to detect and stop misuse of accounts.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract for the Session itself (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and the Operator's legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure for the IP address and user agent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Briefs, Feedback and Versions {#data-writing}

- **What:** the Briefs and Feedback you write, and every Version Pumpkit writes from them, including the Sketch it starts from and the Version's text.
- **Why:** to write your Posts and revise them.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Inspiration authors you pick {#data-inspiration-authors}

- **What:** the X handles you choose as Inspiration authors.
- **Why:** to write your Posts in the style of those authors.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The public posts of those authors are covered in [Inspiration authors](#inspiration-authors).

### Scheduled posts {#data-scheduled-posts}

- **What:** the text of each Scheduled post, its publish time, whether it was Published or Failed and why, and the identifier X gives the published post.
- **Why:** to publish your Scheduled posts on X at the time you choose.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### X connection {#data-x-connection}

- **What:** your X account identifier, handle and the address of its photo, the permissions you granted, your X subscription type, and the access tokens X issues, stored encrypted. Pumpkit shows your X account's photo by having your browser load it from X.
- **Why:** to publish on your X account when you ask Pumpkit to.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Billing {#data-billing}

- **What:** your Stripe customer identifier and the state of your Subscription: its Plan, status and billing periods. Stripe receives your name and email address, and you enter your payment details directly with Stripe. Your card details never reach the Operator.
- **Why:** to charge for your Subscription and keep the accounting records the law requires.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and the Operator's legal obligations under Italian tax law for invoices and accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### Analytics in your browser {#data-analytics}

- **What:** only if you give Analytics consent: the pages you view, what you click, session recordings with every typed input masked, errors in the page, your browser and device type, and the IP address your browser connects from. Once you are signed in, your user identifier, email address and name are linked to this data.
- **Why:** to understand how people use Pumpkit and to find and fix problems.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, as [Cookies and browser storage](#cookies) explains.

### Server events and error reports {#data-server-events}

- **What:** when a Scheduled post is Published or Failed, the server records an event under your user identifier. When the server hits an error, it sends an error report to PostHog. These do not depend on Analytics consent and store nothing in your browser.
- **Why:** to measure whether publishing works and to fix errors.
- **Legal basis:** the Operator's legitimate interest in running a reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Transfers outside the EU {#transfers}

Some recipients process data in the United States or another country outside the European Union. Each such transfer relies on one of two safeguards: the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for a recipient certified under it, or the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, included in the recipient's data processing terms. DigitalOcean and PostHog process data in the European Union, so no transfer applies to them.

To get a copy of the safeguard that covers a transfer, write to support@pumpkit.io.

## Your writing is not used to train models {#no-training}

The Operator does not use your Briefs, Feedback or Versions to train AI models. Pumpkit's OpenRouter account excludes model providers that train on the prompts they receive.

## How long data is kept {#retention}

- **Account content** (your profile, Google identity, Briefs, Feedback, Versions, Inspiration authors, Scheduled posts and X connection): until your account is deleted.
- **Session and Magic link records:** 90 days after they expire. This includes a Magic link requested by someone who never created an account.
- **Inspiration authors' public posts:** while any User has that author among their Inspiration authors, or until the author objects.
- **Billing records held by Stripe, and invoices:** 10 years, as Italian tax law requires.
- **Analytics events, server events and error reports:** 30 days.
- **Session recordings:** 30 days.
- **Backups:** backups of the server are overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted data leaves them within 30 days.

## Your rights {#rights}

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data the Operator holds about you and get a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected (rectification);
- have your data deleted (erasure);
- restrict how your data is processed;
- receive the data you gave the Operator in a machine-readable format and have it sent to another provider (portability);
- object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, write to support@pumpkit.io from the email address of your account. The Operator answers within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

### Deleting your account {#account-deletion}

To delete your account, write to support@pumpkit.io from the email address of your account. The Operator deletes the account within 30 days. Deleting your account ends any Running Subscription at once, with no refund for the unused period. Billing records stay with Stripe and in the Operator's accounts for the 10 years the law requires, and backups keep the deleted data until they are overwritten, as [How long data is kept](#retention) says.

### Complaints {#complaints}

If you think the Operator processes your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)), or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## Inspiration authors {#inspiration-authors}

This section is for the holders of X accounts that a User picks as an Inspiration author.

When a User picks your X account as an Inspiration author, Pumpkit fetches your recent public posts through twitterapi.io, stores them, and sends them to OpenRouter so that the User's Posts follow your writing style. Your posts set only the style of those Posts, never their content. Pumpkit does not publish your posts and does not contact you.

- **Source:** your public posts on X, read through twitterapi.io.
- **Legal basis:** the legitimate interest of the Operator and its Users in learning writing style from posts you made public (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** while any User has you among their Inspiration authors, or until you object.
- **Recipients:** DigitalOcean, which hosts them, and OpenRouter with the model provider behind it, as listed in [Who receives your data](#recipients).

You can object at any time. Write to support@pumpkit.io with your X handle. The Operator deletes your stored posts and blocks your handle so no User can pick it again. The Operator may ask you to show that the X account is yours.

## Cookies and browser storage {#cookies}

Pumpkit stores a few things in your browser. Some are strictly necessary for Pumpkit to work and need no consent. The others serve analytics and are set only after you give Analytics consent.

### Strictly necessary {#cookies-necessary}

| Name                                                                             | Kind          | Purpose                                                     | Lifetime                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `__Host-refresh_token`                                                           | Cookie        | Keeps you signed in to your Session                         | 30 days                                |
| `google_oauth_sign_in_state`, `google_oauth_nonce`, `google_oauth_code_verifier` | Cookies       | Protect Google sign-in from forgery                         | 10 minutes                             |
| `pumpkit:session`                                                                | Local storage | Holds the access token of your current Session              | Until you sign out or the Session ends |
| `pumpkit:theme`                                                                  | Local storage | Remembers whether you chose the light, dark or system theme | Until you clear it                     |
| `pumpkit:analytics-consent`                                                      | Local storage | Remembers whether you gave Analytics consent                | Until you clear it                     |

### Analytics, only with Analytics consent {#cookies-analytics}

| Name                            | Kind                     | Purpose                                                        | Lifetime                             |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `ph_<project key>_posthog`      | Cookie and local storage | Lets PostHog recognise your browser across visits              | 1 year                               |
| `__ph_opt_in_out_<project key>` | Local storage            | Records that PostHog may capture in this browser               | Until you withdraw Analytics consent |
| PostHog session entries         | Session storage          | Tie a session recording together                               | Until you close the tab              |
| `pumpkit:posthog-identified`    | Session storage          | Records that this tab already linked analytics to your account | Until you close the tab              |

Until you give Analytics consent, Pumpkit does not load PostHog at all: it sends nothing to PostHog and stores nothing for it. If you refuse, Pumpkit works the same.

Analytics traffic goes from your browser to pumpkit.io/ph-relay, a relay on Pumpkit's own server that forwards it to PostHog's servers in the European Union. Because the requests go to pumpkit.io, browser extensions that block analytics domains do not block them. Your browser sends them only after you give Analytics consent.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw Analytics consent, Pumpkit stops sending data to PostHog and deletes what PostHog stored in your browser.

When you subscribe, you complete payment on Stripe Checkout, a page run by Stripe. Stripe's own privacy policy covers the cookies it sets there.
